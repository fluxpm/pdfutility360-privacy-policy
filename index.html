1. Who we are and what this covers

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF Utility 360 (the “App”), provided by [Your name or company] (“we”, “us”), handles information. It applies to the App on Android devices. By installing or using the App you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App.

2. Short version

The App works entirely on your device. We do not collect, receive, store, sell, share or transmit your personal information or your files. The App does not request internet access, has no accounts, and contains no advertising, analytics or tracking. Files only leave the App when you choose to save, export, share or print them.

3. Information we collect

None. We do not collect personal data, device identifiers, location, contacts, usage analytics, crash reports or advertising IDs.

4. How the App handles your files

Opening files. The App opens a PDF or image only when you choose it using Android’s system file picker, or when you open or share a file to the App from another app (for example “Open with” or Share). Files are read and processed on your device.

Scan and photos. If you use Scan, the App asks Android’s own camera app to take a photo. The App itself does not hold camera permission and cannot access your camera. Photos you pick from your gallery or take with the camera are processed on your device to create your PDF. To import them reliably, the App converts each picked image into a temporary JPEG copy in its private storage (see “Temporary files” below).

Password-protected PDFs. If you enter a password to open a protected PDF, it is used only in memory to open that file. It is never stored, logged or transmitted.

Signatures and images. A signature you draw, and images you add to a document, are used only on your device. Up to three signatures you choose to keep are stored locally (see section 6).

Temporary files. To open shared files, take photos, and prepare files for saving, sharing or printing, the App uses private temporary storage that other apps cannot read. These files are removed automatically (when the App next starts, and after about a day at most). You can also clear them any time by clearing the App’s storage.

Results. The App writes a result (a PDF, ZIP of images, or text file) only where you choose to save it, or to the app you choose to share it with, or to the printer service you choose. We never receive a copy.

5. Saving, exporting, sharing and printing

Save and Export write files to the location you pick in Android’s file picker. If you choose a cloud-backed folder (for example a cloud drive), that provider’s own privacy policy applies to the saved file.

Share hands the file to Android’s share sheet and the app you select. That app then handles the file under its own policy. We do not see which app you choose or the file.

Print uses Android’s print system and the print service or printer you select. Some print services process documents in the cloud under their own policies.

Share app and Rate app open Android’s share sheet with a message and a link to our Google Play page, or open that page in the Play Store. The Play Store handles ratings and reviews under Google’s policies.

6. Information stored on your device

The App stores a few items locally, in its private app storage: preferences (theme, preview and output quality, edited-text option), whether you have seen the introduction, and up to three signature images that you chose to keep. They never leave your device. You can remove saved signatures in the App (My signatures → Delete saved), or remove everything by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling it.

7. Permissions

The App does not require special permissions. It does not hold camera, microphone, contacts, location, internet or general storage permissions. File access is limited to files you explicitly select, files you share to the App, and locations you explicitly choose when saving.

8. Third parties

Google Play and Android may collect limited, anonymous diagnostic information (such as crash or performance data) if you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with Google; we may see aggregated reports in the Play Console, but the App itself does not collect or send this data.

11. Security and your responsibilities

Always review a redacted, edited, signed, scanned or compressed file before relying on or sharing it. Copies you save, share, print or export are outside our control once they leave the App.

12. Retention and deletion
